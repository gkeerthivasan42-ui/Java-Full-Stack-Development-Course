History of MangoDB:
===================
MangoDB was created by Eliot and Dwight in 2007,
when they faced scalability issues.
while working with relational database.

the organization that developed MangoDB was originally known as 10gen.

In Feb 2009, they changed their business model and released MangoDB as an open source project.

the organization changed its name in 2013 and now know as MangoDB Inc.

Features of MangoDB:
===================
1. MangoDB provides high performance.
Most of the operations in the MangoDB are faster compared to relational databases.
2. MangoDB provides auto replication features.
that allow you to quickly recover data in case of a failure
3. Horizontal scaling is possible in MangoDB because of sharing.
sharding is partitioning of data and placing it on multiple machines in such a way that the order of the data is preserved.
